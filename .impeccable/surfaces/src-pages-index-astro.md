## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is filed as a technical specification with drawing sheets. Each system is drawn in pen and ink, every part carries a reference numeral, and all the prose belongs to Cam. It refuses the category default of a hero line over a grid of project cards with tech-tag pills.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is bright bond paper (#F7F9FB, cool from optical brightener) with blue-black drafting ink (#191E2E) and graphite secondary ink (#59606E). Non-photo blue (#9CD3EA) is reserved for the working layer: blank rulings, construction lines and guides, all of which disappear in print. The type is STIX Two Text throughout, with spaced capitals for titles, true small caps for labels, lining tabular figures in data and old-style figures in prose; Courier Prime is used only for listings and hashes. Drawings use 1.25px ink strokes, 45° hatching for tone, and curved leader lines ending at numerals. Rules are hairlines. Nothing has rounded corners, shadows or fills of colour.

STORY: The visitor meets Cameron White's name and the blank abstract, sees from the numbered data block what exists (Jexpanel, Falcon Hosting, further work, experience, correspondence), recognises FIG. 1 as a real system, and points at numerals to explore. They come to believe the work is real and inspectable, open a specification, and reach correspondence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A running head carries the handle, the numeral navigation (100–500) and a live sheet counter. Below it, CAMERON WHITE is set in spaced capitals at about 7vw across the full measure, with the (12) role blank at right. The front page has two columns split by an ink rule: on the left, the data block of entries 10–500 with live Jexpanel figures, every entry a link; on the right, the ABSTRACT as four ruled blanks, then FIG. 1 (the Jexpanel deployment) drawn in line with numerals 102–140. The primary action is the (100) entry and the 500 correspondence links, both visible without scrolling at 1440×900.

FORM: Patent specification with drawing sheets used as a document form; position 5 of my seven ranked candidates; seed:0eebc237cd665e3b. Signature interaction: live reference numerals, with hover, focus or click on any numeral lighting every instance through line weight and hatching, and deep links to #ref-N. Motion grammar: figures draw themselves once in a draughtsman's stroke order when first seen; live numbers settle on a damped spring; the numeral and title carry across pages in a cross-document view transition; reduced motion makes all of it static.
